How a detection gets resolved

Every incident moves through the same six stages, with evidence recorded at each one.

01

Detect

Vision model scans authorised CCTV and field-app frames for hazard signatures.

02

Score risk

Severity, location and rainfall context combine into a public-safety priority.

03

Notify

The responsible department gets a geo-tagged alert with a recommended action.

04

Assign

A field team is assigned and given the location, hazard type and severity.

05

Repair

Progress is logged in stages as the crew works the incident on site.

06

Verify

Before/after evidence with timestamp and location closes the incident.
